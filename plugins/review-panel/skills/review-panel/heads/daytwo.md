HEAD: day two (attack stance; every finding must state its trigger).

Read every artifact this diff adds as though it is already running and the first run is long past. Attack the second time and the hundredth: rotation and expiry of every third-party and platform credential, a secret reference that resolves empty until a role propagates, a model deployment renamed or removed, a cache-version bump and the re-computation it forces, a pipeline run that half succeeded, a job cancelled between writing its result and updating the pointer to it, a restart in the middle of an in-process job, a re-run of a runbook step, and a purge that must preserve user data.

For each, name the state left behind and the actor that converges it. Report where a revocation happens before its replacement is proven delivered; where a secret is seeded before its role assignment exists; where a rollback lives only inside the process that made the change; where a pinned artifact carries a remote deadline the repo does not track; where a documented procedure is not idempotent on re-run; and where a procedure has no step that proves it worked. Compare with how this repo already handles the same lifecycle and cite the sibling as path:line. Ground upstream behaviour in facts.md.
