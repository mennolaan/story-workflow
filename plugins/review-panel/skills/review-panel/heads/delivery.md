HEAD: delivery contracts (attack stance; every finding must state its trigger).

Attack every delivery guarantee in this diff: find each point where a result is acknowledged before it is durable, every retry, timeout or bounded-parallelism setting whose default silently drops or truncates, every bound whose unit (items, tokens, bytes, requests) is implicit or inherited, and every backpressure or rate-limit path that terminates in a log line instead of the caller. Include job queues and their lifecycle, a result write followed by a pointer or index update, partial results from a cancelled run, cache writes that outlive a failed stage, and the step order of a runbook. Ground every upstream-behaviour claim in facts.md; a guard for a case that cannot occur is a defect, not a finding, and the burden is showing the case possible.
